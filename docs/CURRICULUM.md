# Aquarium Tycoon: active ESL curriculum

Code-derived inventory, 2026-11-13. Audience: Japanese elementary beginner ESL
children. “Active” means a target used by a current question, purchase, care
prompt or listening-and-tapping activity, including the built outdoor previews.
It does not mean every phrase declared in the answer bank is a correct answer
that the scheduler currently asks for.

The main progression remains exactly eight levels. Coastal & Polar, Dolphin
Lagoon and Grand Ocean Sanctuary remain release-locked; the current coast preview
shows Penguin Coast only. Seal Cove and Otter Bay are documented separately as
built, hidden activities, not as currently available player content.

## Active grammar and vocabulary by category

| Category | Heard/read pattern | Student response or action | Assessed vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colour requests | `What color?`, initially `Blue or orange?`; decoration choices such as `Red or blue?` | `<colour>, please!` | red, blue, yellow, green, orange, pink, purple, white; available colours depend on the shop/animal/light |
| Animal requests | `What do you want?` | `A/An <animal>, please!` | turtle, seahorse, jellyfish, eel, octopus, crab, stingray, parrotfish, shrimp; shark, zebra shark, ray at the tunnel counter |
| Tunnel structure requests | Model sentence is read aloud | `An arch, please!`, `A rock, please!`, `A reef, please!`, `A ledge, please!`, `Sand, please!` | arch, rock, reef, ledge, sand |
| Size requests | `Big or small?` | `Big, please!` / `Small, please!` | big, small |
| First-use care | Model sentence is read aloud | `Feed the fish!`, `Clean, please!` | feed + fish; clean |
| Listening: colour | `I like <colour> fish!`, `I like <colour>!` | Tap a matching fish; later a matching animal or decoration | the eight colours above; fish |
| Listening: animal | `I like <animals>!` | Tap a matching named animal | turtle(s), seahorse(s), jellyfish, eel(s), octopus(es), crab(s), stingray(s), parrotfish, shrimp |
| Identification | `What's this?` | `It's a/an <animal>.` | turtle, seahorse, jellyfish, eel, octopus, crab, stingray, parrotfish, shrimp, shark, ray, seal, sea otter, dolphin, orca, beluga |
| Counting | `How many fish/sharks/penguins/dolphins/orcas?` | `<number> fish/shark(s)/penguin(s)/dolphin(s)/orca(s)!` | one–eight for fish; one–six answer families for sharks, penguins, dolphins and orcas (only the actual population is correct) |
| Colour description | `What color is it?` | `It's <colour>.` | red, blue, yellow, green, orange, pink, purple, white; sanctuary beluga questions assess white |
| Likes: yes/no | `Do you like fish/<animals>?` | `Yes, I do.` / `No, I don't.`; either preference is valid | yes, no; the question supplies the animal noun |
| Likes: favourite colour | `What color do you like?` | `I like <colour>.` | red, blue, yellow, green, orange; pink, purple, white added from Level 6 |
| Size description | `Is it big or small?` | `It's big!` / `It's small!` | big, small |
| Tunnel listening and finding | `Find a shark!`, `Find a ray!`, `Look at the big shark!` | Tap the matching animal | shark, ray, big |

The judge also uses the existing likes pattern with `your aquarium` and `sea
animals`; these are understood in the question, while the answer is assessed on
yes/no. The shared animal identification bank also contains `It's a fish.` and
`It's a penguin.` as distractors; current indoor identification generators choose
named sea animals, and the penguin observation generator asks counting questions.

Speech recognises the existing content words and accent/recogniser variants;
articles, `please`, `I like` and sentence word order are not independently graded.
The same answer IDs supply mic-free tap choices. Listening/find tasks assess an
animal or colour tap rather than spoken production.

Fish counting normally selects tanks of 2–8 single fish; a still-valid waiting
question can also have one fish. Mixed animal tanks and tanks with a school are
excluded. Nine and ten remain in the number answer/judging bank (and can appear
as displayed alternatives), but no current generator makes them correct fish
counts. The tunnel counts every shark, not rays. Outdoor counts use the actual
pod/habitat population, with number tags.

## Active targets by area

Indoor visitor requests are generated from the whole owned indoor aquarium and
can take the camera to another hall. Shops likewise offer animals with a suitable
home anywhere indoors. The animal lists below describe the assessed names that
can refer to occupants of each area's permitted tanks, not separate area-specific
shop catalogues. Colours, counting and requests remain conditional on actual
stock, tank compatibility and level.

| Area | Active question/activity patterns | Area vocabulary and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Hall 1 (A/B) | Colour/size purchases; first-use feed/clean; listening `I like…`; fish counting; likes/favourite-colour chat; named-animal identification; Level 8 colour description | fish; seahorse, jellyfish, eel, octopus, crab, shrimp in compatible later tanks; eight colours; big/small; count within tank capacity, at most five here. Initial tutorial and first judge visit reuse these targets. |
| Jellyfish Gallery (F/G/H) | Shared indoor visitor patterns; animal/colour purchases; display-light `What color?`; decoration size/colour requests; care | fish, seahorse, jellyfish; jellyfish species share the assessed noun `jellyfish`; colours purple/white/blue/orange distinguish jellyfish; light choices are blue, purple, pink, green, orange, white; fish counts at most four in compatible tall/hex tanks |
| Ocean Hall (C/D/E) | Shared indoor visitor patterns; animal/colour purchases; display-light, decoration and care prompts | fish, turtle, seahorse, jellyfish, eel, octopus, crab, stingray, parrotfish, shrimp in compatible tanks; eight colours, big/small; fish counting up to eight |
| Coral Reef Atrium (I/J/K) | Shared indoor visitor patterns; animal/colour purchases; decoration and care prompts | fish, seahorse, eel, octopus, crab, stingray, parrotfish, shrimp; eight colours, big/small. Count only eligible tanks with at most eight single fish; a busy mixed reef or chromis school is not counted. |
| Shark Tunnel | Animal and structure requests; first-use care if not yet learned; `Find a shark/ray!`; `Look at the big shark!`; `What's this?`; `How many sharks?`; `Do you like sharks/rays?` | shark, zebra shark (purchase), ray; arch, rock, reef, ledge, sand; big; one–six sharks; yes/no. Final Tour remains three tasks: find → identify → count. |
| Grand Hub | No assessed language activities | Navigation, map, trophies and decorative visitors only; its small reef fish are scenery, not question targets |
| Penguin Coast (preview) | `How many penguins?` | penguin(s), one–six answer family (normally three then four milestone animals). Animals hold and receive number tags; the water-framing helper temporarily frames the question and restores the selected view. Items/care here are taps, not extra English tests. |
| Seal Cove (built, hidden) | `What's this?` → `It's a seal.` | seal; penguin/sea otter are identification distractors. A visible steady animal is held and ringed. |
| Otter Bay (built, hidden) | `What's this?` → `It's a sea otter.` | sea otter; seal/penguin are identification distractors. The animal is held and ringed. |
| Dolphin Lagoon (preview) | `What's this?`; `How many dolphins?`; `Is it big or small?`; `Do you like dolphins?` | dolphin(s), one–six answer family (current pod: four), big/small from adult/calf comparison, yes/no. Dolphin Discovery keeps four questions: identify → count → size → likes. |
| Grand Ocean Sanctuary (preview) | Orca/beluga `What's this?`; `How many orcas?`; `Is it big or small?`; `Do you like orcas?`; beluga `What color is it?` | orca(s), beluga, one–six orca answer family (current pod: four), big/small, yes/no, white. Beluga questions require the cove to be open. Final tour keeps five questions: orca identification → count → size → beluga identification → likes. |

Coast observation counts, the tunnel milestones, and the outdoor tours keep their
existing task counts. Sanctuary Family and Friends now uses the existing orca
likes question; historical completion of the retired location question still
counts as earned credit.

## Menu/label vocabulary, not assessed targets

item is not an English vocabulary test. Some longer labels contain an already
assessed word (for example `Small tank` contains `small`); the full label is not a
separate target.

Ambient animal likes, `Look!` narration, welfare hints and research stickers
also expose English without assessing it: swimming together, breathing, diving,
jump, mother/baby, looking at us, a clean ocean, Food/Play/Together. Tapping a
Look! moment records an observation; the child is not tested on those words or
on a present-continuous grammar target. Sanctuary improvement/cleanup tasks are
conservation interactions with Japanese guidance, not additional ESL answers.

## Retired targets and source coverage

Animal-ability questions/answers (`Can it…?`, `Yes, it can.`, `No, it can't.`)
and location/preposition questions/answers (`Where is…?`, in/on/under/by/above)
are absent from active prompts, answer mappings and instructional frames. Size
big/small remains active. Ordinary directions, object positions, movement,
`Underwater` view labels and scenery do not introduce assessed prepositions.

Historical IDs load as retired history rather than restoring a question:
`aboveMe`, `byMe`, `yesItCan`, `noItCant`, `onRock`, `inWater`, `onWater`,
`underWater`, `byIsland`, `byBoat`; instructional IDs `where`, `canIt`,
`whereSeal`, `whereDolphin`, `whereOrca`; tunnel activity IDs `where`/`can`;
sanctuary historical question `where`. The saved tunnel `whereDone` field keeps
its old credit and now represents the middle identification step. New completion
calls with retired tunnel or sanctuary question IDs are safe no-ops.

This inventory was derived by following the code's reachable generators and
their answer choices, rather than treating every display string as curriculum:

- `src/game/logic/english.ts`: `PHRASES`, colour/name/count maps and active versus
  saved-only phrase IDs.
- `src/game/logic/sentenceTeaching.ts`: instructional patterns and retired frames.
- `src/game/logic/requests.ts`, `src/game/flow/visitors.ts`: request eligibility,
  question wording, correct answers and tap/number choices.
- `src/game/flow/actions.ts`, `src/game/flow/story.ts`: purchase/care prompts,
  tutorial, first counting question and both judge visits.
- `src/game/logic/model.ts`: display-light colour choices and progress fields.
- `src/game/logic/tunnel.ts`, `src/game/tunnel/activities.ts`: tunnel selection,
  finding, answer generation, milestone credit and three-step Final Tour.
- `src/game/flow/coast.ts`, `src/game/flow/lagoon.ts`,
  `src/game/flow/sanctuary.ts`, `src/game/logic/sanctuary.ts`: outdoor selection,
  answers, tours, Look! narration and milestone requirements.
- `src/game/data/fish.ts`, `decor.ts`, `tanks.ts`, `rooms.ts`, `coast.ts`,
  `hub.ts`, `sanctuary.ts`, `release.ts`: vocabulary, compatible homes, pads,
  landmarks, labels and release/visibility limits.
- `src/ui/CoastHud.tsx`, `LagoonHud.tsx`, `SanctuaryHud.tsx`, `Settings.tsx`,
  `Screens.tsx`: menu and viewpoint labels; `src/game/logic/save.ts` and
  `saveRecovery.test.ts`: historical ID preservation.
